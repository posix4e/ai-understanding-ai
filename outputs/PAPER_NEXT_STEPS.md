# Which next steps come from the papers?

**Yes—several broader next steps are explicitly suggested by the authors. Our exact 105-layout experiment was our own design.** None of these four papers prescribes that experiment, its six seeds, its thresholds, or its complete set of alternative masks.

## What was our own contribution to the plan?

The papers supplied precedents and warnings. We designed the **complete 105-order restoration class, the 2,415-order boundary panel, the six fresh-model confirmation, the exhaustive matched-mask comparisons, and the public prediction freeze** for this project. These are applications of the literature plus deductions from this model's architecture and our earlier results.

Our proposed controls matched for removed attention mass, repairs that infer pair relationships without being given them, and an external replication are **our recommendations**. They are reasonable responses to the remaining limits, but we should not attribute those exact experiments to these authors.

There is also a timing distinction: the October 1 Geng paper was read after E4 confirmation. It informed the later literature assessment and could guide E5; it did not supply E4's already frozen design. The earlier [dated literature update](LITERATURE_UPDATE_2026-10-03.md) records that boundary.

For the website, a concise description is: **“Our next steps follow directions in recent papers: test explanations against actual behavior, check the choice of intervention, and move beyond small synthetic tasks. Our exhaustive input-order study is our own concrete test of those ideas.”**
