# Publication assessment: completed research package

3 October 2026. This assessment closes the experimental and manuscript work in
the earlier [publication plan](PUBLICATION_PLAN.md). That frozen plan is retained
in its original pre-confirmation form.

The package supports a focused paper on prospective transfer of a supplied
attention repair in a finite synthetic setting. It does not establish priority
over the wider literature, acceptance by a venue, or a general AI capability.

| Planned requirement | Delivered evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Precise restoration class and separation from behavior | [Frozen theory](experiment5/THEORY.md): 105 of 2,520 layouts, missing-source counterexample, residual key-state route |
| Prospective fresh-model test with complete controls | [Public registration](https://github.com/posix4e/ai-understanding-ai/commit/de745644b5c71853755e8d53cc9b3dec2abdc2ed), six fixed new seeds, 96 remotely verified files, 624 alternative-mask conditions |

The main result is approximate behavioral recovery across all six new models
and all 105 primary layouts. The correct guard outperforms the specified mean
matched alternatives. This is stronger evidence than fitting an explanation to
one familiar model and layout. Two severe dictionary counterexamples remain,
despite very high overall accuracy. The separate boundary test also shows that
successful behavior need not require exact native-state restoration.

The paper reports the failed stronger edge-magnitude prediction. It does not
replace its registered threshold with a weaker positive-direction claim. The
boundary sample contains only 256 shared dictionaries, and repeated interventions
do not create millions of independent examples. Supplied pair identities and
canonical position IDs remain central limitations.

Before submission to a specific venue, responsible human researchers must review
the science, take authorship responsibility, supply affiliations and disclosures,
and apply that venue's format. External replication and broader architectures
are useful next studies; they are not results delivered by this experiment.
